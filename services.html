What We Do

Financial planning, not just product-selling.

Every engagement starts with your goals — retirement, a child's education, a home — and works backward to the right mix of instruments.

Mutual Funds & SIPs

Fund selection and systematic investment plans built around your timeline and risk comfort, not the flavour of the month.

Tax-Saving Investments

ELSS, PPF and other instruments chosen to fit your tax slab first, and your long-term wealth goals second — not the other way round.

Life & Health Insurance

Adequate term and health cover reviewed against your actual liabilities and family size — no over-selling, no under-insuring.

Retirement Planning

A clear number for the corpus you'll need, and a funded plan to get there — reviewed as income and expenses change.

Child's Education Planning

Goal-dated investments that account for rising education costs, sized to be ready well before the deadline arrives.

Wealth & Portfolio Review

An annual, unhurried review of everything you hold — rebalancing where needed, consolidating where it helps.
